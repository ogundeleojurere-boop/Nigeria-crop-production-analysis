# Nigeria Crop Production Analysis (2015–2024)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the production of five selected crops in Nigeria—Cassava, Maize, Rice, Sorghum, and Yams—from 2015 to 2024 using agricultural data from FAOSTAT.

The analysis was carried out in Microsoft Excel to identify production trends, compare crop contributions, examine changes over time, and present the results through tables, calculations, and visualizations.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze production patterns among the selected crops, identify the crops with the highest and lowest production, compare production between 2015 and 2024, and examine the overall trend in combined production during the ten-year period.

## Dataset

**Source:** FAOSTAT
**Country:** Nigeria
**Period:** 2015–2024
**Measure:** Production
**Unit:** Tonnes

### Crops Analysed

* Cassava, fresh
* Maize (corn)

* Sorghum
* Yams

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

* Data filtering and organization
* Total production calculations
* Percentage contribution by crop
* Year-to-year production comparisons
* Identification of highest and lowest production
* Combined production analysis by year
* Formula-based analysis using Excel
* Tables and charts for data visualization

## Key Findings

* Combined production of the five selected crops increased from **125,627,575 tonnes in 2015** to **154,740,878 tonnes in 2024**, representing an overall increase of **23.17%**.
* **Cassava, fresh** recorded the highest total production over the 2015–2024 period, with approximately **602.14 million tonnes**.
* **Yams** recorded approximately **555.91 million tonnes** over the period.
* Cassava and Yams accounted for **42.29%** and **39.05%** of combined production respectively.
* Together, Cassava and Yams accounted for **81.34%** of the combined production of the five selected crops.
* **Sorghum** recorded the lowest share of combined production at **4.78%**.
* Between 2015 and 2024, production increased for Cassava, Maize, Rice, and Yams, while Sorghum recorded a decrease.
* Yams recorded the largest percentage increase between 2015 and 2024 among the five selected crops.

## Conclusion

The analysis of Nigeria's production of five selected crops—Cassava, Maize, Rice, Sorghum, and Yams—from 2015 to 2024 shows an overall increase in combined production. Production increased from 125,627,575 tonnes in 2015 to 154,740,878 tonnes in 2024, representing an overall increase of 23.17%.

Cassava and Yams accounted for the largest shares of total production during the period, contributing 42.29% and 39.05%, respectively. Together, they accounted for 81.34% of the combined production of the five selected crops, while Sorghum recorded the lowest share at 4.78%.

The comparison between 2015 and 2024 shows that production increased for Cassava, Maize, Rice, and Yams, while Sorghum recorded a decrease. Yams recorded the largest percentage increase between the two years, while Sorghum was the only crop among the five to experience a decline.

Overall, the analysis demonstrates how Excel can be used to organize agricultural production data, calculate totals and percentages, identify production patterns, compare years and crops, and present findings through tables and charts. The results provide a clear summary of production patterns for the selected crops in Nigeria over the ten-year period.
